A careful school archive

School photographs are part of a child's educational record and must be shared responsibly.

Official images only

This preview pairs supplied photographs with draft captions. The published archive will show only photographs approved by the school, with consent recorded; unrelated stock images will not be used.

Consent and care

Images of students will be published only with appropriate consent and according to the school's safeguarding practice.

Accurate captions

Every photograph will carry a clear caption, date and accessible description so the archive remains useful and inclusive.
