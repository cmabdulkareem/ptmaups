About our school

A school rooted in Bedira, open to the future.

For families in Bedira and the surrounding communities, the school is a place of steady learning, shared responsibility and everyday encouragement.

Established in 1976, Panakkad Thangal Memorial AUP School has grown with its community. The school's work is shaped by a simple belief: education should prepare young people not only for examinations, but for life beyond the classroom.

Visitors to this website can learn about the school's academic approach, student activities, facilities and admissions. Official information that is still awaiting confirmation is clearly marked throughout the site.
