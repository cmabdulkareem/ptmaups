Who we are

About our school

Panakkad Thangal Memorial Aided Upper Primary School is an educational institution located in Bedira, Chala, near Vidyanagar, in Kasaragod district, Kerala.

School-directory records identify the institution as a government-aided, co-educational school serving the primary and upper-primary levels.

Established in 1976, the school has a long-standing presence in its local community. Its role is to support children's educational development during their formative years and help them build the knowledge, skills, and values needed for the future.

Our educational purpose

Opportunity for every child

We believe every child deserves opportunities to learn, ask questions, express ideas, develop friendships, and discover individual strengths.

Our educational approach should encourage students to develop foundational academic skills alongside communication, creativity, cooperation, self-discipline, and social responsibility.

Our purpose

What every child deserves

  • Opportunities to learn and ask questions
  • Space to express ideas
  • Friendships within the school community
  • The chance to discover individual strengths

Learning and development

Building foundations for the future

The primary and upper-primary years are important stages in a child's development. During these years, students build the foundations for further learning, develop independent thinking, and learn to participate confidently in their community.

More than classroom instruction

A positive school experience combines classroom instruction with opportunities for exploration, collaboration, cultural expression, and practical learning.

Our school community

A shared responsibility for young people

A school is strengthened by the people who contribute to it. Students, teachers, parents, school leadership, and community members all have a role in supporting an effective learning environment. We recognise the importance of cooperation between home and school in encouraging attendance, learning, well-being, and student development.
