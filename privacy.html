Privacy

Privacy policy

How this website works, what information it does and does not handle, and what to expect from demonstration features.

In summary

No form data is sent

Enquiries are validated in your browser only

Last updated: 25 October 2026

1. Purpose of this website

This is the informational website of Panakkad Thangal Memorial AUP School, Bedira, Kasaragod, Kerala, India. Its purpose is to share general information about the school with students, parents, staff, prospective families and the wider community.

This policy describes the website as it currently operates. It does not describe systems that may be introduced later, unless those features are explicitly noted as future plans.

2. Information you enter into forms

The admission enquiry and contact forms on this website are demonstrations. When you submit either form, your entries are checked in your own browser to show how validation works. The information is not transmitted to the school, not saved on a server and not sent by email.

  • No server or database receives the form fields.
  • No email is generated from the form.
  • The form is cleared from the page after a successful demonstration.

Please do not enter sensitive personal information into these demonstration forms. For official enquiries, contact the school office using confirmed contact details once they are published.

3. Announcement preference

The announcement bar can be dismissed. To remember that choice, the website stores a small preference value in your browser's local storage. This value identifies the announcement that was dismissed so that the bar remains hidden until the message changes.

This information stays in your browser. It is not readable by the school through this website, and it is not shared with anyone. You can remove it at any time by clearing site data in your browser settings. A new announcement message will appear even if an earlier one was dismissed.

4. Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently set cookies, run analytics, use advertising trackers or create user profiles. There is no account system, no newsletter registration and no comment facility.

5. School content and photographs

Notices, events, calendar entries and gallery photographs are maintained by the school in a separate WordPress publishing system. The public website is static: an automated sync reads the published updates and approved photographs from a public WordPress content feed, copies them into the website, and updates the pages. Visitors do not interact with WordPress and no visitor information is sent to it.

The content feed and the published media files are publicly readable so that the static site can be rebuilt without storing WordPress credentials. They contain only published, approved school updates and approved photographs; drafts, private entries, consent references and account information are not included. Only content approved for public display should be entered in WordPress.

Photographs of students are published only after consent has been recorded in WordPress. Each photograph also needs a consent reference and alternative text before an automated check allows it to appear. The consent reference is kept in WordPress and is not published. Personal or confidential information should not be entered in WordPress posts, photograph filenames, captions or image metadata.

6. External resources

The website requests typefaces from Google Fonts to keep the design readable and consistent. When those files are requested, your browser connects to Google's servers, which may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details. This is a normal characteristic of loading an external font. The website falls back to system fonts if the service is unavailable.

No other third-party scripts or embeds are currently used. If an approved map or other external service is added later, this policy should be updated before it is enabled.

7. Links to other websites

The website may link to external sites in future, for example an official school system. Those sites operate under their own privacy policies. The school is not responsible for the content or practices of external websites.

8. Children's privacy

The website is intended for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The demonstration forms do not transmit or store information. Parents and guardians should supervise any online enquiry made on behalf of a student.

9. Data security and retention

Because no personal information is transmitted or stored through this website, there is no server-side retention of form entries. The announcement preference remains only in your browser until you clear it. If official online services are added later, appropriate security and retention arrangements should be documented here.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy will be reviewed when the website changes, particularly if real contact forms, approved maps, analytics or other data-handling features are introduced. The updated date at the top of this page will change with any revision.

11. Contacting the school

Questions about this website or a request relating to official school records should be directed to the school office. The complete postal address, official telephone number, email address and office hours are awaiting confirmation on this website and are not invented here.
