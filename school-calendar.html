    How this calendar works

    A clear view of the school year

    The calendar displays the school's confirmed academic dates, holidays and events. Entries are published here as they are confirmed by the school office.

    Monthly navigation

    Move between months to see the entries recorded for each one. Use the Today button to return to the current month.

    Date details

    Select any date to read the entries recorded for that day. Dates without entries are shown clearly.

    Category labels

    Entries are labelled by category in the date details and the month list, so the type of each date is clear at a glance.

    Calendar updates

    Keeping up with school dates

    Academic dates, holidays and examination schedules are published here as they are confirmed. Notices about calendar changes are also published on the notice board.

    Contact the school office
